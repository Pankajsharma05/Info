Research Summary

Research focuses on topological superconductivity and hybrid quantum systems where superconductivity, magnetism, and topology interplay. Work is based on the Bogoliubov–de Gennes formalism and numerical simulations to investigate Majorana bound states, Josephson transport, and spin textures in quantum materials. Recent studies explore altermagnet–superconductor heterostructures and skyrmion-induced transport phenomena.
